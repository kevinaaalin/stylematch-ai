# Purpose

The TIGI Product Engineering Master Plan defines the official engineering documentation system of TIGI.

This document is the root document of the entire engineering knowledge system.

It does not describe product implementation.

It defines:

- document governance
- engineering principles
- document hierarchy
- ownership
- traceability
- cross references
- canonical identifiers
- lifecycle

All implementation specifications shall be maintained in their own engineering documents.

---

# Engineering Philosophy

TIGI adopts Product Engineering instead of Software Documentation.

Every specification is treated as an engineering asset.

Every engineering asset has:

- ownership
- lifecycle
- version
- dependency
- canonical identifier

No duplicated specification is allowed.

---

# Engineering Principles

## EP-01

Single Source of Truth (SSOT)

Every engineering concept shall have only one authoritative document.

---

## EP-02

Single Responsibility

Every engineering document owns only one responsibility.

No mixed specifications.

---

## EP-03

Cross Reference

Documents reference each other.

Documents never duplicate each other.

---

## EP-04

Canonical Identifier

Every engineering artifact owns a permanent identifier.

Identifiers never change.

---

## EP-05

Traceability

Every requirement must be traceable.

Requirement

↓

Design

↓

Implementation

↓

Testing

↓

Release

---

## EP-06

Governance First

Governance specifications override implementation decisions.

Implementation cannot violate Governance.

---

## EP-07

Platform Independence

Engineering specifications remain independent of programming language.

---

## EP-08

Technology Independence

Documents describe engineering behavior rather than framework implementation.

---

## EP-09

Extensibility

Every engineering component shall support future extension without modifying historical specifications.

---

## EP-10

Official Edition Policy

Released engineering books become immutable.

Changes require version upgrade.

Historical editions remain archived.
