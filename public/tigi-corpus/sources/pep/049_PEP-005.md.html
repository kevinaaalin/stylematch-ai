```sql
workflow_id BIGINT,

inspection_status VARCHAR(30),
```

---

```sql
CREATE TABLE inspection_item(

item_id BIGINT PRIMARY KEY,

inspection_id BIGINT,

gs_code VARCHAR(20),

item_name VARCHAR(200),
```
